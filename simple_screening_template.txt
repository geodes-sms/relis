=== EVALUATION INSTRUCTIONS ===

1. **Assessment:** Evaluate the paper against ALL inclusion and exclusion criteria.

2. **Inclusion Decision:** A paper should be INCLUDED if:
   - It meets ALL relevant inclusion criteria
   - It does NOT violate any exclusion criteria
   - It is relevant to the review topic

3. **Exclusion Decision:** A paper should be EXCLUDED if:
   - It fails to meet any critical inclusion criteria
   - It violates any exclusion criteria
   - It is clearly irrelevant to the review topic

4. **Output Format:** Provide your decision in the following JSON format:
```json
{
  "decision": "INCLUDE" or "EXCLUDE",
  "confidence": [0-10 scale],
  "reasoning": "Brief explanation of your decision"
}
```

5. **Important Notes:**
   - Be CONSISTENT in your evaluation
   - Be THOROUGH in checking all criteria
   - Be LENIENT when in doubt (better to include than exclude)
   - Confidence should reflect how certain you are about your decision
